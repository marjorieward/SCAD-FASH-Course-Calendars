Before Class:

  • Bring the following pattern pieces (cut, notched, and marks transferred) to class:
    • Machine Sewing Practice Pattern (2 pieces)
  • Bring the following materials to class:
    • Laptop, tablet or note-taking supplies
    • Juki Industrial Bobbin
    • Juki Industrial Bobbin Case
    • DBx1 sewing needles 90/14
    • Large Flat Head Screwdriver
    • Hand Sewing Needles
    • All-purpose thread in desired color(s)
    • Medium weight muslin
    • Clear ruler with 1/8 inch markings
    • Frixion brand pen(s)
    • Ball head quilter's pins
    • Fabric Scissors
    • Paper Scissors
    • Transfer Paper
    • Serrated Transfer Wheel
    • Seam Ripper
    • Tape Measure
    • Thread snips/embroidery scissors (optional)

During Class:

  • How to thread, operate, maintain, and troubleshoot a sewing machine.
  • Work on machine sewing practice sample.

After Class:

  • Complete hand sewing and machine sewing samples.
  • Pin and cut all sample book patterns from muslin, interfacing, and contrast fabric.
